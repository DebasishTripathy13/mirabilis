Ten `lm run` generations of the same prompt, warm server, performance
governor, ~28 GiB RAM available against a 28.1 GiB model.

  20.7  23.6  24.0  24.2  22.8      (first five)
  19.5  23.2  23.0  24.5  25.0      (second five)

  sorted: 19.5 20.7 22.8 23.0 23.2 23.6 24.0 24.2 24.5 25.0
  median: 23.4 tok/s
  range:  19.5 - 25.0

The README quotes the median. `lm tune` reports 26.06 for the same
configuration, because it measures short bursts and keeps the best of
several runs -- the right estimator for comparing candidates against each
other, and the wrong one for advertising what a session feels like.

The spread is the 0.3-0.4 GiB of model that cannot stay cached on this
machine: runs differ in how much of it they re-read from disk.
